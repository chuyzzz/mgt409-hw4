# Usability notes

- Navigation exposes Home, Products, About Us, login, and account creation.
- Product cards are keyboard-accessible links and open the full detail view.
- The chat widget echoes the shopper’s submitted message and displays structured catalogue results above the page.
- Seasonal filters narrow the catalogue without a page reload.
- Sale prices retain the original price and discount label.
- Auth forms provide validation and clear success/error messages.
- Product images use containment so garments are not cropped by their background.
